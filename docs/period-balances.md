# Period Balances

Period balances store the month-end asset, liability, and equity figures for a `YYYYMM` period after you have checked reconciliation.

## Register after reconciliation looks right

1. Open **Reconciliation** and confirm the period looks correct.
2. Open **Period Balances**.
3. Select the same period.
4. Review the proposed figures taken from the reconciliation computation.
5. Choose **Register** when you are ready.

The page links back to reconciliation so you can verify again before saving. You can register even if the period is still marked unbalanced, once you accept the figures.

## Stored month-end figures

Registration stores the month-end totals for that period. After a successful save, the registered balance card shows the stored figures and when they were last updated.

## Overwrite with history

If a balance is already registered for the period, registering again opens a confirmation dialog. It compares the existing figures with the newly proposed ones. Confirming overwrites the stored month-end balance and keeps history of the change.

## Separate from Manage Balances

Period balances are **not** the same as the per-account snapshots you enter in **Manage Balances** on the Accounts page.

| Feature | What it stores |
| --- | --- |
| Manage Balances | Per-account recorded balances on specific dates |
| Period Balances | Whole-period month-end asset / liability / equity totals |

Use Manage Balances while working through accounts; use Period Balances to lock in the month after reconciliation.
